Puzzle Block Blast ("the game") is made by [developer / company name] ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

## Information stored on your device
Your settings, game progress, level stars, achievements, statistics and an in-progress Classic game are stored on your device. They are not sent anywhere unless cloud backup is active (below).

## Cloud backup
When the game's online services are enabled, your progress (level results, daily challenge results, achievements, best scores and gameplay counters) is backed up to Google Firebase under an anonymous account the game creates for you. There is no name, email or contact information. It is used only to restore and sync your progress. You can ask us to delete it at [contact].

## Analytics and crash reports
If you allow it (Settings → Privacy, on by default), the game sends anonymous gameplay events, such as a game started or a level completed with scores in ranges, and crash reports to Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics). These help us fix bugs and improve the game. They do not include your name or contacts, and the game does not collect your advertising ID for analytics. You can turn either off at any time in Settings.

## Advertising
The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested, the game asks for your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice later under Settings → "Ad privacy choices". AdMob may collect device identifiers and other information as described in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Ads you choose to watch for an extra undo or hint are optional.

## Purchases
"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. To confirm a purchase, our server checks it with Google Play and records it against your anonymous account.

- Turn analytics and crash reports off in Settings → Privacy.
- Change ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
