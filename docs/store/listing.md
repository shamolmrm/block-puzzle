**Full description:**
Fit the blocks. Clear the lines. Puzzle Block Blast is a relaxing, thinking block puzzle you can play anywhere, even offline.

• **Classic:** place pieces on a 10×10 board, clear full rows and columns, and chain clears for combo points. How long can you keep space open?
• **50 levels:** hand-made puzzles with goals: clear lines, reach a score or collect gems. Every level has been proven solvable. Earn up to 3 stars by winning in fewer moves.
• **Daily Challenge:** a fresh puzzle every day, the same for everyone, playable offline. Keep your streak going!
• **Progress:** gain XP, level up and unlock 13 achievements. Track your stats.
• **Fair by design:** no fake difficulty and no pay-to-win. Undo and hints are free, with optional extra ones from ads you choose to watch.
• **Comfortable:** sound and vibration options, a reduce-motion mode, large-text support and screen-reader labels.

The game contains ads. A one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns off ads between games.
