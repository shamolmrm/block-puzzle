BlockFit Puzzle by BahaLabs

BlockFit Puzzle has no sign-up and no accounts with names or emails. It keeps your game progress on your device and an anonymous backup of it in the cloud. You can delete both at any time, free of charge.

Delete the cloud backup

  1. Email hamidakhan7654@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data – BlockFit Puzzle".
  2. Tell us roughly when you installed the game, so we can find your anonymous backup.
  3. We delete the backup and its anonymous account within 30 days and confirm by email.
Request deletion by email

Delete data on your device

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → BlockFit Puzzle → Storage and tap Clear storage, or
  2. Uninstall the game. This removes all progress and settings stored on the device.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your progress backup (level results, stars, scores, achievements and gameplay counters) and the anonymous account it belongs to. Nothing is kept afterwards.
  • Analytics and crash reports: these hold no personal details and are deleted automatically by Google (analytics after up to 14 months, crash reports after up to 90 days). You can stop them at any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • Advertising data: managed by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
